# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Simple Interest Calculator project!

All contributions, bug reports, bug fixes, documentation improvements, enhancements, and ideas are welcome. We appreciate your efforts to improve this open-source project!

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork** this repository to your GitHub account.
2. **Clone** your fork to your local machine.
3. **Create a new branch** for your proposed changes.
4. **Make your changes** and test them thoroughly to ensure everything works as expected.
5. **Commit your changes** with a clear and descriptive commit message.
6. **Push your branch** to your forked repository on GitHub.
7. **Open a pull request** to this repository, explaining your changes and their purpose.
8. **Wait for review and approval.** Once your contribution is accepted and merged, your work will be acknowledged through GitHub's contribution history.

## Reporting Bugs

If you discover a bug, please open a GitHub Issue.

Include:
- A clear description of the problem, including screenshots if applicable.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- Expected behavior versus actual behavior.
- Any relevant error messages or additional context.

## Suggesting Enhancements

Suggestions for improving the Simple Interest Calculator are always welcome!

Please open a GitHub Issue describing:
- Your proposed enhancement.
- Why the enhancement would be useful.
- How it could improve the project's functionality or user experience.

## Code of Conduct

All contributors are expected to follow our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative environment.
